## Company snapshot
S&P Global sells ratings (Ratings), financial data and workflow tools (Market Intelligence, MI), commodity price benchmarks (Energy), and index licences (Indices). Mobility (auto data) was spun off on 2026-07-01 as Mobility Global, NYSE: MBGL (UNVERIFIED (web): Mondo Visione, 2026-07-01). It sits at the crossing of data and old-industry plumbing: every bond, ETF and commodity contract passes through its benchmarks.

## Bull / Bear
**Bull**
- Margin is earned, not cyclical: +10 points of GAAP operating margin in two years survives stripping the one-offs (10-k:458, 465), and costs grew slower than revenue (10-k:535, 453).
- Issuance cyclicality is smaller than it sounds: the 20.5% transaction bucket fell back from its FY2024 spike and revenue still grew 8% (10-k:577, 453). A 20% fall in issuance costs ~$1.0-1.1 of EPS, ~6% (Ratings-transaction base ~$3.0B to 10-K transaction bucket $3,144M) (valuation agent, assumptions: 65% flow-through, 22.5% tax, 295.5M shares).
- The guide cut is a spin-off basis change: organic growth 6-8% held and Ratings/Indices were raised (sentiment agent, 2026-07-28, UNVERIFIED (web)). Indices asset-linked fees +13% in Q4 2025 (call:168) and +22% in Q2 2026 (web).
**Bear**
- This is a rates/macro-linked earner by construction: Ratings is 46% of FY2025 segment profit (10-k:850-867); management itself guided issuance growth negative for Q4 2026 and bank loans soft (call:134, 190). A 30% issuance fall cuts EPS ~$1.5-1.6 to ~$16, below the $17.50 bar (valuation agent). The investor avoids Fed-dependent theses; this is the part that is.
- Incremental growth comes from the market-linked lines (Indices, transaction), not contracts: the 2026 guide assumes 5-7% equity appreciation (call:182), and asset-linked fees follow index levels and passive flows. The sources contain no ETF fee-trend data.
- AI risk lands on MI, whose organic growth guide is only 5.5-7% and whose segment margin is 16-20% after amortization (10-k:1011, 850-879; call:188). The 10-K names AI and free public data as substitutes (10-k:214, 230); management did not size AI's 2026 contribution (call:320). Shares fell to a 2-year low in Feb 2026 on AI fears (UNVERIFIED (web): Investing.com, 2026-02).
- The bull's hardest-to-counter point is the bear's too: the ~65% subscription-plus-non-transaction share is computed, not labelled by the company, and the retention data needed to prove switching costs is missing.

## Valuation
Lens: P/E plus reverse DCF on adjusted EPS ex-Mobility (headline GAAP is distorted by the spin and disposal gains).
- Price $386.27 on 2026-10-02 (UNVERIFIED (web): Market Chameleon; 15 days old, no live quote). 294.8M shares outstanding x $386.27 = $113.9B market cap (UNVERIFIED (web): Q2 2026 release; matches quoted $113.87B). Net debt ~$11.0B, EV ~$124.9B.
- P/E 21.9x on the $17.625 guide midpoint ($386.27 / $17.625); earnings yield 4.6%; gross buyback ~$7B / $113.9B = 6.2%.
- Implied expectation: ~9% a year EPS growth at a constant multiple for a 9% return (assumption), i.e. ~$22.8 by FY2029. FY2027 consensus $19.32 is +9.6% over the guide midpoint (UNVERIFIED (web): MarketBeat, 2026-07-20). Q4 2025 adjusted EPS grew 14% (call:146); a full-year growth rate is not stated in the local call.
- **Verdict: Deserved**, resting on delivered growth above the ~9% the price needs, but only while issuance and index levels hold.
- **Falsifying number** ("Deserved" is disproved by a number BELOW a bar): FY2026 adjusted diluted EPS ex-Mobility **below $17.50** (USD per share); current guide $17.50-17.75, midpoint $17.625; deadline Q4 2026 release (~Feb 2027). The verdict rests on this guide, which is itself a web figure (UNVERIFIED (web)).

## Kill conditions
Event-based; bounded issuance damage (~6-9% of EPS) sits in Bear because it is far below the ~50% threshold.
1. **[open-ended]** MI organic cc growth below 4% for two consecutive quarters (baseline 5.5-7%, call:188) together with a disclosed AI-driven seat or renewal loss. No retention baseline exists to size it, so it has no visible floor. Primary kill condition: the strongest bear point with no bull counter (AI substitution of data/research, 10-k:214, 230).
2. **[open-ended]** Loss or weakening of regulatory recognition or benchmark standing for ratings/indices (10-k:356-374), or a shift of passive products away from S&P DJI indices. The 10-K names the risk; its size is not quantifiable from the sources.
Not a kill condition, a verdict test: FY2026 adjusted EPS below $17.50 (Valuation).

## What to ask
1. What is the like-for-like FY2025 ex-Mobility revenue and EPS base, so the $17.50-17.75 guide can be read as growth rather than a perimeter change?
2. What are MI's retention/renewal and net-expansion numbers by product, and how many seats have AI tools removed? (Without them, switching costs stay an assumption.)
3. How much of Indices' growth comes from fee-bearing net inflows vs index-level appreciation, and what happens to the 2026 asset-linked guide if equity markets are flat?
